### Final answer
As of April 7, 2025, the CFO of Airbnb (NASDAQ: ABNB) is Ellie Mertz.

Reasoning and supporting facts:
- On December 4, 2023, Airbnb's Board appointed Ellie Mertz as CFO effective March 1, 2024 (the "CFO Transition Date"), when she was the company's Vice President of Finance (SEC Form 8-K, FY2023).
- She succeeded Dave Stephenson, who was moved to the newly created Chief Business Officer role effective January 1, 2024 (SEC Form 8-K; CNBC, Dec. 5, 2023).
- Airbnb's Investor Relations page lists her as Chief Financial Officer and says she oversees the global finance team, including financial planning and analysis, accounting, tax, treasury, and investor relations.
- A DoorDash board filing confirms she has served as Airbnb's CFO since March 2024.
