### Final answer
**Answer: Ellie Mertz is Airbnb's Chief Financial Officer as of April 7, 2025.**

**Appointment timeline**
- Airbnb's board named her CFO on December 4, 2023, with the appointment taking effect on a set transition date. The 8-K filing states that the board appointed her as CFO effective as of the CFO Transition Date.
- That transition date was March 1, 2024. Her predecessor, Dave Stephenson, stopped serving as CFO at that point and continued as Chief Business Officer.
- DoorDash's board profile for her says she has been CFO at Airbnb since March 2024, after serving as Airbnb's Vice President of Finance from January 2019 to March 2024.

**Scope of role**
Airbnb's investor relations page says she leads the company's global finance team. Her responsibilities include financial planning and analysis, accounting, tax, treasury and investor relations.
