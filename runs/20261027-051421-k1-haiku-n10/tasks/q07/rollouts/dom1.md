### Final answer
**Answer: Ellie (Elinor B.) Mertz is Airbnb's Chief Financial Officer as of April 7, 2025.**

**Supporting details:**
- Airbnb's investor relations site identifies Mertz as the current CFO and says her remit covers the global finance team, including financial planning, accounting, tax, treasury and investor relations.
- The company's December 2023 Form 8-K shows the board named her CFO effective March 1, 2024 (the "CFO Transition Date"). Dave Stephenson, who had been CFO, stayed on through that date as Chief Business Officer.
- Before becoming CFO, she was Airbnb's Vice President of Finance from January 2019 to March 2024. She joined Airbnb in 2013 after several finance roles at Netflix from 2006 to 2013.
