### Final answer
As of April 7, 2025, the CFO of Airbnb (NASDAQ: ABNB) is Ellie Mertz (full name Elinor Mertz).

Reasoning:
1. The board appointed her CFO in a December 2023 SEC 8-K, effective on a CFO Transition Date of March 1, 2024. Dave Stephenson, the prior CFO, became Chief Business Officer. The filing states: "the Board appointed Ellie Mertz as the Company's Chief Financial Officer effective as of the CFO Transition Date."
2. She was still CFO in early 2025. A Form S-8 signed February 13, 2025 lists "Elinor Mertz" as Chief Financial Officer (Principal Financial Officer).
3. Her remit covers the global finance team, including financial planning and analysis, accounting, tax, treasury and investor relations.
4. Before becoming CFO she was Airbnb's Vice President of Finance. Before joining Airbnb in 2013 she spent 2006–2013 in finance roles at Netflix.
