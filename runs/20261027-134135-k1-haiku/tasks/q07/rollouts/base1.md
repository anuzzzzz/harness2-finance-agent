### Final answer
As of April 07, 2025, the CFO of Airbnb (NASDAQ: ABNB) was Elinor "Ellie" Mertz.

Basis:
- The Board appointed her CFO in December 2023, effective on a later transition date. (SEC Form 8-K, December 2023: "Also on December 4, 2023, the Board appointed Ellie Mertz as the Company's Chief Financial Officer effective as of the CFO Transition Date.")
- Before that, she was Vice President of Finance, responsible for strategic finance and analytics, corporate planning, and investor relations.
- Her tenure as CFO began in March 2024 (per the company's DEF 14A proxy: "Mertz has served as the Company's Chief Financial Officer since March 2024"). That proxy is dated after April 7, 2025, but it only confirms a start date that had already passed.
- She oversees the global finance team, including financial planning and analysis, accounting, tax, treasury, and investor relations (Airbnb Investor Relations page).

Later-dated sources (e.g., her June 2025 Netflix board appointment and 2025 SEC filings) were excluded because they postdate the question's date.
