### Final answer
**Ranking of FY2024 GAAP dividend payout ratios (highest to lowest):** Keurig Dr Pepper (~84.76%) > Coca-Cola (~78.63%) > PepsiCo (~76.69%) > Monster Beverage (0%)

**Method:** Payout ratio = dividends ÷ GAAP earnings. Coca-Cola uses aggregate dollar figures from its 10-K. PepsiCo and KDP use per-share figures because I could not retrieve their aggregate dividends paid. Both bases are noted below.

**Coca-Cola (KO)**
- Cash dividends paid were $8,359 million in 2024, versus $7,952 million in 2023 (<cite index="3-1">The Company paid dividends of $8,359 million and $7,952 million during the years ended December 31, 2024 and 2023</cite>).
- Net income attributable to Coca-Cola shareowners was $10,631 million for 2024 (<cite index="40-1">Net Income Attributable to Shareowners of The Coca-Cola Company 10631</cite>).
- Payout = 8,359 ÷ 10,631 = **78.63%**.
- Per-share cross-check: the annualized 2024 dividend was $1.94 per share (<cite index="3-4">Our annualized common stock dividend was $1.94 per share and $1.84 per share in 2024 and 2023</cite>). Dividing by 2024 diluted EPS of about $2.46 (not in my sources) gives roughly 78.9%, so the ranking holds.

**PepsiCo (PEP)**
- Dividends declared per share for 2024 were $5.33 (per the Fidelity dividend table: <cite index="17-4">2024 | 5.3300 | 0.08</cite>).
- 2024 diluted EPS was $6.95 (<cite index="48-1">Diluted net income attributable to PepsiCo per common share $ 6.95</cite>).
- Payout = 5.33 ÷ 6.95 = **76.69%**, on a per-share basis.

**Keurig Dr Pepper (KDP)**
- Dividends declared in 2024 were $0.215 in February and May, then $0.23 in September and December. Nine-month declared dividends were $0.66 (<cite index="20-3">total dividends of $0.66 per share and $0.615 per share for the first nine months of 2024 and 2023</cite>), so the full year is $0.66 + $0.23 = $0.89.
- GAAP diluted EPS for 2024 was $1.05 (<cite index="62-10">Diluted EPS decreased 32.3% to $1.05 per diluted share as compared to $1.55 in the prior year.</cite>), which was depressed by a goodwill impairment.
- Payout = 0.89 ÷ 1.05 = **84.76%**, on a per-share basis.
- On adjusted diluted EPS of $1.92 (<cite index="64-6">Adjusted diluted EPS increased 7.8% to $1.92</cite>), the ratio would be about 46.35%. The GAAP figure is inflated by the impairment, so the adjusted figure is a useful counterpoint.

**Monster Beverage (MNST)**
- Monster does not pay a dividend (<cite index="36-3">To date, Monster Beverage Corp has not paid any dividends.</cite>), so its payout ratio is **0%**.

**Caveats**
- The ranking depends on the earnings basis. On GAAP EPS, KDP ranks first. On adjusted EPS, KDP drops to roughly 46%, below Coca-Cola and PepsiCo.
- PepsiCo and KDP are per-share approximations, not aggregate dividends divided by net income.
- Third-party TTM payout figures (for example, MarketBeat and Investing.com) reflect later periods, so I did not use them for FY2024.
