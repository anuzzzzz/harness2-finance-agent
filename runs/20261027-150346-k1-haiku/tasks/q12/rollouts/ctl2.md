### Final answer
**Answer: On the assumptions below, TSMC's implied Q1 2025 revenue is about NT$825.11 billion (≈US$25.16 billion at the guidance FX rate). That is inside the guided range, above the low end by about 0.62%, and below the midpoint by about 0.96%. So it beats the bottom of the guidance but misses the midpoint.**

Date note: the question is framed as March 11, 2025, but I was told to answer as of April 7, 2025. Either way, March 2025 revenue had not been reported yet, so I projected it. I did not use the actual March figure, which was published after the reference date.

**1. Q1 2025 guidance**
- In its January 16, 2025 release, TSMC guided <cite index="61-9">revenue to be between US$25.0 billion and US$25.8 billion; and, based on the exchange rate assumption of 1 US dollar to 32.8 NT dollars</cite>.
- On February 10, 2025, TSMC said <cite index="32-6">the revenue forecast for the first quarter of 2025 is now anticipated to be closer to the lower end of the guidance range</cite> after an earthquake.
- Guidance in NT$ (US$ × 32.8):
  - Low: 25.0 × 32.8 = NT$820.00B
  - Midpoint: 25.4 × 32.8 = NT$833.12B
  - High: 25.8 × 32.8 = NT$846.24B

**2. Actual monthly revenue so far**
- January 2025: <cite index="14-2">NT$293.29 billion</cite> (NT$293,288 million).
- February 2025: <cite index="3-3">NT$260.01 billion, a decrease of 11.3 percent from January 2025</cite> (NT$260,009 million).
- Jan + Feb 2025 = NT$553.297B.

**3. Historical March growth (month-over-month vs. February)**
The question is ambiguous, but "March growth rate" is taken as March vs. February, and I used the three most recent years (2022–2024).
- March 2022: <cite index="51-3">NT$171.97 billion, an increase of 17.0 percent from February 2022</cite>. Calculated: 171,967 / 146,933 − 1 = +17.04%.
- March 2023: <cite index="43-1">NT$145.41 billion, a decrease of 10.9 percent from February 2023</cite>. Calculated: 145,408 / 163,174 − 1 = −10.89%.
- March 2024: <cite index="22-3">NT$195.21 billion, an increase of 7.5 percent from February 2024</cite>. Calculated: 195,211 / 181,648 − 1 = +7.47%.

**4. Average March growth**
- (17.0377% − 10.8878% + 7.4661%) / 3 = 13.6160% / 3 = **+4.54%** (4.5387%).
- Using the rounded published rates (17.0, −10.9, 7.5) gives 4.53%, which is essentially the same.

**5. Projected March 2025 revenue**
- 260,009 × (1 + 0.045387) = 260,009 + 11,801 ≈ **NT$271,810 million (NT$271.81B)**.

**6. Projected Q1 2025 revenue**
- 293,288 + 260,009 + 271,810 = **NT$825,107 million (NT$825.11B)**.
- In USD at 32.8: 825.107 / 32.8 ≈ **US$25.16B**.

**7. Beat or miss**
- vs. low end (NT$820.00B): **+NT$5.11B (+0.62%)**, a beat.
- vs. midpoint (NT$833.12B; US$25.40B): **−NT$8.01B (−0.96%)**, a miss. In USD, that is about −US$0.24B.
- vs. high end (NT$846.24B): −NT$21.13B (−2.50%).

**Conclusion:** Under the stated assumptions, TSMC lands within the US$25.0–25.8B guidance, toward the low end. It beats the bottom of the range but misses the midpoint by about 0.96%. This is consistent with management's February comment that results would be closer to the lower end.

**Caveats:** The projection is only as good as three data points, and March growth is volatile (+17.0%, −10.9%, +7.5%). Using year-over-year March growth instead would give a different answer.
