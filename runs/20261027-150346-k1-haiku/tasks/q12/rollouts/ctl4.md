### Final answer

**Bottom line:** Using the 3-year average March growth rate, TSMC's implied Q1 2025 revenue is about NT$782.33 billion, or roughly US$23.85 billion. That is a **miss** of about US$1.15 billion (4.59%) below the low end of guidance (US$25.0B), and about US$1.55 billion (6.10%) below the midpoint (US$25.4B).

**Date note:** The question says March 11, 2025, but I was instructed to answer as of April 7, 2025. On that date the March 2025 revenue report (due April 10) is not yet out, so March is projected, not known. I used only data available as of that date.

**1. Q1 2025 guidance**
- On January 16, 2025, TSMC guided Q1 revenue to <cite index="16-2">between US$25.0 billion and US$25.8 billion</cite>, <cite index="16-2">based on the exchange rate assumption of 1 US dollar to 32.8 NT dollars</cite>.
- On February 10, 2025, TSMC said that after the January earthquake, <cite index="11-6">the revenue forecast for the first quarter of 2025 is now anticipated to be closer to the lower end of the guidance range</cite>. This makes the low end the more relevant benchmark.
- Guidance in NT$ at 32.8: low = 25.0 × 32.8 = NT$820.00B; high = 25.8 × 32.8 = NT$846.24B; midpoint = 25.4 × 32.8 = NT$833.12B.

**2. Actual Jan and Feb 2025 revenue**
- <cite index="3-3">Revenue for February 2025 was approximately NT$260.01 billion</cite>, and <cite index="3-4">revenue for January through February 2025 totaled NT$553.30 billion</cite>. Precisely, Jan–Feb = NT$553,297M.

**3. Historical March growth rates (year-over-year)**
- March 2021: NT$129,127M (<cite index="41-3">an increase of 13.7 percent from March 2020</cite>, per the March 2021 report).
- March 2022: NT$171,967M, <cite index="42-3">an increase of 33.2 percent from March 2021</cite>.
- March 2023: NT$145,408M, <cite index="27-3">a decrease of 15.4 percent from March 2022</cite>.
- March 2024: NT$195,211M, <cite index="22-3">an increase of 34.3 percent from March 2023</cite>.

Calculations (unrounded):
- 2022: 171,967 / 129,127 − 1 = 33.18%
- 2023: 145,408 / 171,967 − 1 = −15.44%
- 2024: 195,211 / 145,408 − 1 = 34.25%

**4. Average March growth (past 3 years: 2022–2024)**
- (33.18% − 15.44% + 34.25%) / 3 = 51.98% / 3 = **17.33%**

**5. Projected March 2025 and implied Q1**
- Projected March 2025 = 195,211 × 1.1733 ≈ NT$229,036M (≈ NT$229.04B).
- Implied Q1 2025 = 553,297 + 229,036 = NT$782,333M ≈ **NT$782.33B**.
- Converted at the guidance rate of 32.8: 782.33 / 32.8 ≈ **US$23.85B**.

**6. Beat or miss**
- Versus the low end (US$25.0B / NT$820.00B): miss by US$1.15B (NT$37.67B), or −4.59%.
- Versus the midpoint (US$25.4B / NT$833.12B): miss by US$1.55B, or −6.10%.
- Versus the high end (US$25.8B): miss by US$1.95B, or −7.56%.

**Sensitivity:** To reach the low end (NT$820.00B), March would need NT$266.70B, which is about 36.62% year-over-year growth. The 17.33% average is well short of that. The average is also pulled down by the 2023 decline, so it is a rough estimate.

**Sources:** TSMC February 2025 revenue report; TSMC January 2025 revenue report (earthquake update); TSMC Q4 2024 earnings release (Q1 guidance); TSMC March 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024 revenue reports.
