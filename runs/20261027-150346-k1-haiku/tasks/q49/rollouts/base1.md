### Final answer
Answer (as of April 7, 2025):

1) Beats of full-year adjusted diluted EPS guidance: 2 out of 2 (FY2023 and FY2024).
- FY2023: Guidance was raised during the year, reaching 8% to 9% constant-currency growth by the Q3 update. Actual adjusted diluted EPS of $4.30 was up 10% in constant currency, above the final range. Beat.
- FY2024: Guidance started at 4% to 6% growth, was narrowed to 4% to 5% in December 2023, and was reaffirmed in March 2024. Actual adjusted diluted EPS of $4.52 was up 6% in constant currency, above the range. Beat. The Q4 FY2024 adjusted EPS and full-year adjusted EPS also topped the consensus estimates reported at the time ($4.50 full-year consensus).
- Caveat: This count uses the final annual guidance as the benchmark. I did not verify quarterly consensus beats for every quarter of FY2023 and FY2024, so I cannot give a reliable quarterly count.

2) FY2025 guidance (as of April 7, 2025): Following the March 19, 2025 Q3 update, adjusted operating profit and adjusted diluted EPS are expected to be down 8% to down 7% in constant currency, versus the prior ranges of down 4% to down 2%. Organic net sales are expected to be down 2% to down 1.5%. Applying the EPS range to the FY2024 base of $4.52 gives an approximate range of $4.16 to $4.20 (4.52 × 0.92 = 4.1584; 4.52 × 0.93 = 4.2036). This is an approximation because guidance is stated in constant-currency percentage terms.
