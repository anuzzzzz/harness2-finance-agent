**Answer: General Mills beat its full-year Adjusted Diluted EPS guidance in both FY2023 and FY2024 (2 of 2). For FY2025, as of April 7, 2025, guidance is for adjusted diluted EPS to decline 7% to 8% in constant currency.**

I counted a "beat" as the full-year constant-currency adjusted diluted EPS growth coming in above the top of the most recent guidance range. I did not use quarterly results versus analyst consensus, since those are not company guidance. I also used only information available as of April 7, 2025.

**FY2023 (beat)**
- In March 2023, <cite index="43-7">Adjusted diluted EPS is now expected to increase 8 to 9 percent in constant currency, compared to the previous range of up 7 to 8 percent.</cite>
- The full-year result was that <cite index="25-3">adjusted diluted EPS of $4.30 was up 10 percent in constant currency</cite>.
- That is about 1 point above the top of the final 8% to 9% range.

**FY2024 (beat)**
- In December 2023, the company <cite index="14-8">expected Adjusted EPS to increase 4% to 5%, compared to the previous range of 4% to 6%.</cite> The company had earlier <cite index="17-2">narrowed its profit and EPS expectations within its original guidance ranges</cite>.
- The full-year result was that <cite index="10-1">adjusted diluted EPS of $4.52 was up 6 percent in constant currency</cite>.
- That is about 1 point above the top of the final 4% to 5% range. The company's CEO said it <cite index="10-3">delivered on our updated guidance in fiscal 2024</cite>, but the reported 6% figure is above the stated range.

**FY2025 guidance (as of April 7, 2025)**
- In the March 19, 2025 third-quarter release, <cite index="32-2">adjusted operating profit and adjusted diluted EPS are now expected to be down 8 percent to down 7 percent in constant currency, compared to the previous ranges of between down 4</cite> (the earlier range was down 4% to down 3%).
