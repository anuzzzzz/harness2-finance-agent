### Final answer
As of April 07, 2025, the CFO of Airbnb (NASDAQ: ABNB) is Ellie Mertz.

Reasoning and supporting facts:
- On December 4, 2023, Airbnb's board appointed Ellie Mertz as CFO effective March 1, 2024 (the "CFO Transition Date"). She was then the company's Vice President of Finance, and she had joined Airbnb in 2013 after several finance roles at Netflix. Dave Stephenson, the prior CFO, stopped serving in that role but remained with the company as Chief Business Officer.
- Airbnb's investor relations site lists her as Chief Financial Officer and states that she oversees the global finance team, including financial planning and analysis, accounting, tax, treasury, and investor relations.
