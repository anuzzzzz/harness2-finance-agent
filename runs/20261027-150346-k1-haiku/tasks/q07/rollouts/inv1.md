### Final answer
Answer: Ellie Mertz is Airbnb's Chief Financial Officer as of April 7, 2025.

Supporting logic and evidence:

1. Appointment: The Airbnb board's December 2023 decision named Ellie Mertz to the CFO post, with the change taking effect on the "CFO Transition Date" (March 1, 2024). Trade coverage confirms she moved into the role on March 1, 2024 (Skift). Because that date precedes April 7, 2025 and no later change appears in the sources I reviewed, she held the role on the question's date.

2. Predecessor: Dave Stephenson, who had been CFO since 2019, was moved to a newly created Chief Business Officer role. He kept the CFO title until the handover and then stopped serving as CFO while continuing in the CBO role (SEC 8-K, Dec. 2023; CFO Dive).

3. Background: Before becoming CFO, Mertz was Airbnb's Vice President of Finance. She had joined Airbnb in 2013 after several finance roles at Netflix from 2006 to 2013 (SEC 8-K; Airbnb Investor Relations).

4. Scope of role: Per Airbnb Investor Relations, she oversees the global finance team, including financial planning and analysis, accounting, tax, treasury, and investor relations.
