### Final answer
**Answer: Production at the McCalla, Alabama facility was expected to begin in calendar year 2025.**

Supporting details:
- In November 2021, the company announced a $1.1 billion investment in a manufacturing facility and distribution center in McCalla dedicated to Uncrustables sandwiches, with <cite index="2-4">construction of the facility expected to begin no later than January 2022, with production commencing in calendar year 2025.</cite>
- Later SEC filings kept the same timing. The fiscal 2022 10-K said <cite index="6-7">construction of this facility began in the third quarter of 2022, with production expected to begin in calen[dar year 2025]</cite>. The fiscal 2024 10-Q (quarter ended January 31, 2024) said <cite index="4-2">construction of this facility began in 2022, with production expected to begin in 2025.</cite> It also said <cite index="4-5,4-6">property damage was incurred at the new manufacturing facility as a result of an equipment fire</cite>, but that the company <cite index="4-7">did not anticipate a significant delay in beginning production in 2025.</cite>

Important update: Production timing has effectively already occurred. Business Alabama reported that <cite index="9-11,9-12">the $1.1 billion J.M. Smucker plant opened today with a ribbon-cutting ceremony in McCalla. Kay Ivey was among the dignitaries attending the opening of the 900,000-square-foot facility</cite> (dated November 7, 2024). The company's 2024 Corporate Impact Report likewise states that <cite index="8-9">"this year, we opened our latest manufacturing and distribution facility dedicated to the production of Uncrustables in McCalla, Alabama."</cite> So the 2025 expectation was the guidance; the facility was opened in late 2024, ahead of that schedule.
