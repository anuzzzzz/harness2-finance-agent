### Final answer
**Answer: Amazon (AMZN) plans to spend the most on capex in 2025, at roughly $100 billion.** Alphabet (GOOG) is second at about $75 billion, and Meta (META) is last at $60-65 billion. These figures are the guidance each company had given as of April 7, 2025.

- **Amazon (about $100B):** Amazon's management projected capex of about $100 billion for 2025, up from roughly $83 billion the prior year, with most of it aimed at AI. Amazon's CEO tied the run-rate to Q4 2024 spending: Amazon spent $26.3 billion in capex in Q4 2024, which he called reasonably representative of quarterly spending in 2025. Figures differ slightly across sources because of how capex is defined.

- **Alphabet (about $75B):** Alphabet said it expects to invest approximately $75 billion in capital expenditures in 2025. The spending goes mainly to technical infrastructure, primarily servers, followed by data centers and networking.

- **Meta ($60-65B):** Meta's initial 2025 guidance was a capex range of $60-65 billion. It said growth would be driven by investment supporting both generative AI efforts and its core business.

**Comparison:**
- Amazon vs. Alphabet: $100B − $75B = $25B more, or 33.33% above Alphabet's plan.
- Amazon vs. Meta: at the top of Meta's range ($65B), Amazon's plan is about 53.85% higher. At the midpoint ($62.5B), it is 60.00% higher.

**Caveat:** Amazon's $100B is described as "more than" or "around" $100B, so the exact figure is approximate. Meta's and Alphabet's guidance could be revised, but this answer reflects the figures as of April 7, 2025.
