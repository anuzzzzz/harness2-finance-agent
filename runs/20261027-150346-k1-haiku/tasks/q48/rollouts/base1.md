### Final answer
**Short answer:** Lemonade's FY2024 results landed at or slightly better than the full-year guidance it gave in its prior quarterly update (Q3 2024, issued October 30, 2024). Every metric beat or met the top of its range: in-force premium (IFP) hit the top end, gross earned premium (GEP) and revenue beat the top end, and the Adjusted EBITDA loss was slightly smaller than the top end of the range.

**Prior guidance (Q3 2024 letter, FY2024 column):**
- The company said it was <cite index="11-10">increasing full year guidance for gross earned premium and revenue, and reaffirming guidance for FY 2024 for all other metrics.</cite>
- Full-year ranges: IFP $940–$944M, GEP $823–$826M, revenue $522–$524M, and Adjusted EBITDA loss ($155)–($151)M. (<cite index="4-2">Gross Earned Premium (GEP) $222 $225 $823 $826 Revenue $144 $146 $522 $524 Adjusted EBITDA Loss ($29) ($25) ($155) ($151)</cite>)

**Actual FY2024 results vs. guidance:**
- **In-force premium:** <cite index="6-16,6-17">ending 2024 with IFP of $944 million, representing 26% year on year growth, and an Adj. EBITDA loss of ($150) million, 13% year on year improvement.</cite> This is at the top of the $940–$944M range.
- **Gross earned premium:** Not stated as a full-year figure in the sources I found. Summing the four quarterly GEP figures ($187.9M Q1 + $199.9M Q2 + $213.1M Q3 + $226.4M Q4 = $827.3M, my calculation) gives a total above the $823–$826M range. The quarterly inputs come from the <cite index="24-6">Q1 2024 letter</cite>, the <cite index="22-6">Q2 2025 letter's comparative 2024 column</cite>, the <cite index="26-1">Q3 2024 letter</cite>, and the <cite index="20-3">Q4 2024 letter</cite>.
- **Revenue:** Full-year total revenue was <cite index="30-3">$526.5 million</cite>, up 22% from $429.8M in 2023. This is above the $522–$524M guidance range.
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** The loss was ($150)M, against guidance of ($155)–($151)M. That is about $1M better than the top of the range and about $3M better than the midpoint.
