**Answer: Beat by about 29 bps.** Lyft's Q4'24 Adjusted EBITDA margin of about 2.64% came in above the 2.35% guidance midpoint.

**1. Guidance (given with Q3'24 results):** Q4'24 outlook was Gross Bookings of $4.28B–$4.35B and Adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 2.3% to 2.4%. Midpoint = (2.3% + 2.4%)/2 = 2.35%.

**2. Q4'24 actuals:**
- Gross Bookings: $4.28 billion (earnings call transcript).
- Adjusted EBITDA (derived; no Q4 dollar figure found in sources): FY2024 Adjusted EBITDA $382.4M minus 9M 2024 $269.6M = $112.8M.
- FY2024 Gross Bookings $16,099.4M minus 9M $11,820.5M = $4,278.9M.
- Q4 margin = 112.8 / 4,278.9 = 2.64% (2.6361%). Management's headline Q4 margin is 2.6%, consistent with this.

**3. Comparison:** 2.64% − 2.35% = +0.29 pp ≈ **+29 bps beat**.
- Using the rounded 2.6% headline: +25 bps (still a beat).
