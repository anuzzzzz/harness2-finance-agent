**Answer: Beat. Lyft's Q4'24 Adjusted EBITDA margin came in at about 2.64% (reported as 2.6%), against a guided midpoint of 2.35%. That is a beat of roughly 29 bps (about 25 bps on the rounded 2.6% figure).**

The midpoint of the margin range is (2.3% + 2.4%) / 2 = 2.35%.

- Unrounded margin: $112.8M / $4,280M = 2.6355%
