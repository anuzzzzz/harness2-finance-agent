### Final answer
As of April 7, 2025, Delta's latest quarterly release was its December quarter 2024 report (January 2025). That outlook covered four metrics: total revenue growth, operating margin, earnings per share, and free cash flow. It also set full-year targets for leverage and pre-tax income.

**Quarterly guidance (next quarter)**
Delta's forecast table for the coming quarter lists three items: year-over-year total revenue growth, operating margin, and EPS. The September 2024 release used this same layout for the December 2024 quarter. The January 2025 release gave the March 2025 quarter a 7% to 9% revenue growth range, a 6% to 8% operating margin range, and EPS of $0.70 to $1.00. The EPS and margin figures are on a non-GAAP (adjusted) basis.

**Full-year 2025 guidance**
The full-year table covered three items: EPS growth, free cash flow, and gross leverage. Delta expected 2025 EPS above $7.35, which it described as more than 10% growth over a normalized 2024 base. It projected free cash flow above $4 billion and gross leverage of 2x or less. Delta's commentary also pointed to pre-tax income above $6 billion for 2025.

**Definitions and caveats**
- Revenue guidance is stated as a percentage change from the prior year, not as a dollar amount.
- Operating margin is operating income divided by operating revenue (my own definition, not from the release).
- Free cash flow equals operating cash flow less gross capital expenditures. For 2024, Delta reported $8.0 billion of adjusted operating cash flow and $4.8 billion of gross capex, which gives $3.4 billion of free cash flow.
- The 2024 EPS base is adjusted for the roughly 45-cent impact of the CrowdStrike-related outage in the September 2024 quarter.
- I excluded Delta's later releases (for example, the April 9, 2025 March-quarter report) because they post-date the reference date.
