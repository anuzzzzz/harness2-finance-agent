# Tool use

- Decide the document and period before searching: company, form (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K EX-99.1) and fiscal period. edgar_search takes a numeric CIK, not a ticker (a ticker returns "Unknown ticker"). Get the CIK from a search-result URL or the filing itself. Never send an empty or null query to web_search. If it returns nothing, retry once with a short non-empty query, then use edgar_search or a known primary URL.
- A search snippet or result title is a lead, not evidence. Open the primary document with parse_html_page (one clear key per document), then ask retrieve_information one targeted question at a time. Use input_character_ranges on long filings. Confirm the figure, period, unit and basis in the retrieved text.
- Program-to-date totals: when a question asks for a total "incurred as of" a date for a named program (merger restructuring, integration, synergy costs), look first for the company's own cumulative statement in the latest 10-K (MD&A business overview or the restructuring note). Quote that sentence with its date. Treat the sum of annual income-statement lines as a cross-check only. If the two differ, report the company's stated total as the answer, show the sum alongside it with periods and units, and say plainly that the filing does not reconcile them. Check that the scope of each figure matches the question. Expected cash expenditures and separate impairment lines are different figures.
- parse_html_page cannot read PDFs or spreadsheets. Use the HTML filing or exhibit.
- Cite only documents you opened and retrieved from. Do not list a filing, Financial_Report.xlsx, article or earlier-year 10-K seen only in search results, and do not attribute a figure to a document you did not retrieve it from.
- Write the final answer in plain text with no citation markup such as <cite> tags. State the figure with its period and unit, where it appears in the filing, any cross-check and the source of each figure. Re-check the submitted figure against the retrieved text, then call submit_final_result.
