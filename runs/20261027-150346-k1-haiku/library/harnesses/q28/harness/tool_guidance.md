# Tool use

- Decide the document and period before searching: company, form, fiscal period. For a "material cash requirements / contractual obligations" question, that is the latest 10-K on or before the as-of date (April 07, 2025), MD&A "Liquidity and Capital Resources". Use edgar_search with the CIK or ticker, form_types and date filters. Do not use the question's wording as the query. Never send a web_search with an empty or null query. If a search returns nothing, retry once with a short non-empty query, then use edgar_search or a known URL.
- A search snippet or result title is a lead, not evidence. Open the filing with parse_html_page (one clear key), then ask retrieve_information one targeted question at a time. For a long 10-K, use input_character_ranges around the liquidity section. Retrieve the full table: every row, every column (total, next 12 months, beyond 12 months), the units and the printed total row. Do not submit figures that came only from a snippet.
- Before submitting, re-add the rows you are reporting and compare the sum with the printed total. Check units (thousands vs millions) and that the period column matches the question ("2025" means the next-12-months column in a table dated December 31, 2024). A successful tool call does not make a figure correct. Also retrieve the table's footnotes and state briefly any obligation the filing says is outside the table (for example unknown future content titles, or unrecognized tax benefits), noting that actual outflows may be larger.
- Cite only documents you opened and retrieved from. Do not list a spreadsheet, PDF mirror or search-result page you did not open. Write the answer in plain text with no citation markup such as <cite> tags, and show the arithmetic. Submit with submit_final_result as soon as the figures are verified.
